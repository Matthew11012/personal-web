UQuizzle won the UQCS Hackathon 2024 — first out of more than 30 teams and 180-plus participants, built over a single weekend with four teammates.

### What it did

It’s a browser extension that sits on top of Echo360 lecture playback and generates quizzes and notes in real time as the recording plays, using React on the front end and the Vercel AI SDK to drive the generation. The idea was to turn a passive recording into something you’re actually being tested on as you watch it.

### Working inside the clock

A hackathon weekend doesn’t leave room for the architecture you’d pick with more time — every decision got made against a clock, not a roadmap. Scoping the extension around one well-understood surface, Echo360 playback, instead of trying to support every lecture platform, is what made a working demo possible by the deadline.
