Siasisten is a teaching-assistant management platform I helped redesign and rebuild in a 12-person team working under SCRUM. The new frontend is ReactJS, the backend is Django REST Framework — both cleaner and more maintainable than the version they replaced.

Working inside a larger team meant scope discipline and clear handoffs between frontend and backend. The redesign focused on the UX friction points that teaching assistants actually hit, not guessed-at problems, which is why the architecture shifted too.
