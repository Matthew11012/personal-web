Every cache is a small wager: that whatever you just computed will be asked for again before it goes stale. Get the horizon wrong and you're either serving ghosts or recomputing everything twice.

The interesting design work isn't the lookup — it's deciding how long to trust the bet, and what happens the moment it's wrong. See also [[rag-query-volume|Cutting RAG query volume by 99.8%]], which this fed into.
