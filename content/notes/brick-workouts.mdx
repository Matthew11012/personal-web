A brick workout — bike straight into a run — is the fastest way to find out what your actual run fitness is, independent of what your legs feel like on a fresh Tuesday.

The first ten minutes are always ugly. What matters is how quickly they stop being ugly, a pattern that showed up again at [[first-olympic-tri|my first Olympic-distance tri]].
