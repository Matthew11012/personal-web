The swim was chaos — a washing machine of elbows for the first four hundred meters before anyone found space to breathe. The bike was math: watts, gearing, and the slow discipline of not going out too hard.

The run was honesty. Whatever pace I’d actually trained at showed up, whether I wanted it to or not.
