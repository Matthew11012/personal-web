Splitting attention across Jakarta and Brisbane doesn't cut a day in two — it just means there are two overlapping half-days, and neither one lines up with when you'd naturally do your best thinking.

Most of what I've written about [[jakarta-brisbane|living between Jakarta and Brisbane]] started as a note-to-self about which hours I actually trust myself to be sharp in.
