Jakarta is home in the way a childhood bedroom is home — familiar down to the smell of the stairwell, even if I don't live in it anymore. Brisbane is home in the way a good apartment is home: chosen, comfortable, still a little provisional.

I used to think I had to pick one. Now I just keep two toothbrushes and stop apologizing for the accent that shifts depending on who's asking.
