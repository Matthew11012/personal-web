Watching a white belt throw a technique wrong is the fastest way to notice you've been doing a smaller version of the same mistake for years. Explaining a stance out loud forces you to actually understand it, not just perform it.

Half of what I now correct in my own sparring, I first caught while correcting someone else's — see [[black-belt-debugging|what a black belt taught me about debugging]].
