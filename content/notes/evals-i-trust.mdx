Most eval suites quietly measure how well a model performs on the eval, not on the product it's wired into. The ones I trust are built from real failures we've already shipped and regretted.

That's the same lesson as [[rag-query-volume|cutting query volume]]: the real signal was always sitting in production, not in a benchmark.
