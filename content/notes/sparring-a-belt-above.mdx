I ask to spar people a belt above me on purpose, knowing I’ll mostly lose. Losing to someone faster tells you exactly where your reaction time actually is, not where you’d like it to be.

It’s the same discipline as debugging under pressure in [[black-belt-debugging|what a black belt taught me about debugging]]: let the faster opponent show you the gap before you pretend it isn’t there.
